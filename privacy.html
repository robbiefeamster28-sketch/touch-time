Touch Time Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

In short: Touch Time does not collect, sell or share your personal information. Your training progress stays on your device. We only count anonymous usage, like how many times the app is opened.

What the app stores

To show your progress, Touch Time saves the following only on your device:

  • Account details you type in: first and last name, profile name, and an optional email. The password and email are stored only as scrambled fingerprints, never as the password or email itself.
  • Time spent on drills, test scores, favorite drills, practices you build, and your daily goal and avatar.

This information is not sent to us or to anyone else. We have no servers that receive it.

Backups

If you choose Download backup, the app creates a file with your account details (including the password fingerprint) and progress, and lets you decide where to save or send it. You can use it to sign in on another device. That file goes only where you send it, so keep it somewhere private.

Online saving on claude.ai

If you use the Touch Time page on claude.ai while signed in, your progress is also saved to your own private storage there, so it is available on your other devices. That storage is provided by Anthropic under the Anthropic Privacy Policy. The Android and iPhone apps do not use it.

Anonymous usage statistics

To see how many people use Touch Time, the app sends anonymous counts to GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly statistics service. It counts:

  • each time the website or app is opened;
  • when an account is created, a drill or practice is finished, or a test is taken (only that it happened).

Along with each count, GoatCounter receives the information every website receives: browser and device type, screen size, the country worked out from the internet address, and the page that linked here. GoatCounter uses this to tell visits apart without storing the internet address, and uses no cookies.

Never sent: names, emails, passwords, scores, training times, favorites or any other progress. The counts can't identify you and aren't used to track you across other apps or websites.

To opt out on the website, turn on "Do Not Track" in your browser settings, and Touch Time won't send anything.

No ads or tracking

Touch Time has no advertising and does not track you. It does not use your location, camera, microphone or contacts.

Children

Touch Time is suitable for young players. Because it collects no personal information, it does not knowingly collect data from children.

Deleting your data

Open Account and choose Deactivate account (your password is needed) to remove the account and all its progress from the device. Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page. Questions: open an issue at github.com/robbiefeamster28-sketch/touch-time.
